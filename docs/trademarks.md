# The CargoFrame name and logo

The code of CargoFrame is free to use, copy, change and share under the MIT License or the
Apache License 2.0 (see [LICENSE-MIT](../LICENSE-MIT) and [LICENSE-APACHE](../LICENSE-APACHE)). The
name **CargoFrame** and the **Convoy** logo (`assets/brand/`, `app/icons/`) are not part of those
licenses: they say where a copy comes from, so that people can trust it.

## What you can do without asking

- Say what your work is built on: "based on CargoFrame", "a fork of CargoFrame", "works with
  CargoFrame", with a link to https://github.com/SotharL/CargoFrame.
- Use the name and logo to talk about CargoFrame itself: an article, a video, a talk, a review, a
  link to the official releases.
- Build and use CargoFrame unchanged, for yourself or inside your company, under its own name.

## What needs another name

- A changed copy that you share with others (a fork, a modified build, a product that includes it)
  needs a name and an icon of its own, so that nobody mistakes it for the official CargoFrame.
  Keep the credit: "based on CargoFrame".
- Do not suggest that the CargoFrame project made, checked or endorses your work.

To ask about anything else, open an issue on https://github.com/SotharL/CargoFrame.

## Cargo and Rust

CargoFrame is written in Rust, but it is not part of the Rust project and is not affiliated with,
or endorsed by, the Rust Foundation. "Rust" and "Cargo" are trademarks of the Rust Foundation. The
"Cargo" in CargoFrame is the English word: what a capture carries along with it.
